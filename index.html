Explore Careers Through Challenges

Instead of starting with a job title, start with a challenge, problem, or opportunity you care about. Use SparkPath Challenge Cards and Path Explorer to discover possibilities, then choose one direction worth investigating.

1. Sort 2. Notice 3. Explore 4. Next step

1. Complete the Challenge Cards

Use SparkPath to sort challenges based on what feels most interesting or meaningful to you. Focus on what catches your attention—not on whether you already know a career connected to it.

Keep in mind: The goal is not to find the “right” career. It is to notice the kinds of problems and opportunities you may want to contribute to.
Open SparkPath & Sign Up ↗

Use code UniversityofIowa when prompted, then complete the Challenge Cards. Return here when you have your top challenges.

2. Notice the pattern

Look across your top challenges. What seems to connect them?

3. Use Path Explorer

Choose one of your top challenges and use SparkPath Path Explorer to see how that challenge connects to real work and learning.

Your research mission: Find one career or job, one organization or company, and one learning opportunity connected to the challenge.

Open Path Explorer from your SparkPath account or Challenge Card results. Access may depend on your school’s SparkPath subscription.

4. Choose your next step

Career exploration gets more useful when you do something with what you learned. Choose the next step that would help you most.
